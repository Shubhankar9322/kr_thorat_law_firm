Practice Areas

K R THORAT LAW FIRM provides legal assistance and representation across a broad range of matters for individuals, families, businesses, and institutions.

01

Property Matters

Property transactions and disputes often involve significant financial investments and complex legal procedures. Whether you are buying, selling, leasing, inheriting, or facing a property dispute, obtaining sound legal advice is essential to safeguard your rights and interests. We provide comprehensive legal assistance to individuals, businesses, developers, and institutions in all aspects of property law, ensuring that every transaction is legally secure and every dispute is handled effectively.

Our services include:

  • Property title verification and legal due diligence
  • Sale, purchase, and transfer of immovable property
  • Drafting and review of sale deeds, lease deeds, gift deeds, and mortgage documents
  • Partition and family property disputes
  • Land ownership and boundary disputes
  • Builder–buyer disputes like RERA, MOFA, Consumer Disputes
  • Inheritance and succession matters
  • Land acquisition and compensation claims
  • Eviction and possession matters
  • Property litigation before courts and authorities
02

Family Matters

Family disputes require not only legal expertise but also sensitivity, discretion, and a practical approach. We understand the emotional and personal nature of family conflicts and strive to resolve them efficiently while protecting our clients' legal rights and long-term interests. Whether through negotiation, mediation, or litigation, we provide compassionate and effective legal representation.

Our services include:

  • Divorce by mutual consent and contested divorce
  • Child custody and visitation rights
  • Maintenance and alimony claims
  • Domestic violence matters
  • Judicial separation and restitution of conjugal rights
  • Adoption and guardianship proceedings
  • Marriage registration and matrimonial advice
  • Dowry-related cases
  • Family property and inheritance disputes
  • Family court representation
03

Civil Matters

Civil disputes can affect your personal, financial, and commercial interests. Our firm provides strategic legal representation in a wide range of civil matters, helping clients resolve disputes through negotiation, mediation, arbitration, or litigation. We focus on delivering practical and effective legal solutions while protecting our clients' rights at every stage of the legal process. Our focus is on delivering timely, effective, and legally sound representation tailored to each client's circumstances.

Our services include:

  • Recovery suits and money claims
  • Contractual and commercial disputes
  • Specific performance suits
  • Injunctions and declaratory suits
  • Consumer disputes
  • Partnership and business disputes
  • Landlord and tenant matters
  • Compensation and damages claims
  • Civil appeals and revisions
  • Representation before civil courts and tribunals
04

Criminal Matters

Facing criminal proceedings can have serious personal and professional consequences. We provide strong legal representation to individuals accused of criminal offences as well as victims seeking justice. From investigation to trial and appeal, we ensure that our clients receive fair representation, strategic legal advice, and a vigorous defence while protecting their constitutional and legal rights.

Our services include:

  • Criminal defence in trial and appellate courts
  • Bail applications, including anticipatory and regular bail
  • FIR registration and quashing petitions
  • White-collar crime and financial fraud matters
  • Cheating, forgery, and criminal breach of trust cases
  • Cybercrime offences
  • Domestic violence and related criminal proceedings
  • Cheque bounce cases under the Negotiable Instruments Act
  • Criminal appeals and revisions
  • Legal representation during investigations and court proceedings
05

Corporate Matters

Businesses today operate in a dynamic legal and regulatory environment where proactive legal support is essential for sustainable growth and risk management. We provide comprehensive legal solutions to startups, entrepreneurs, private companies, partnerships, and established organizations, assisting them with corporate governance, regulatory compliance, employment matters, and commercial transactions. Our approach is focused on helping businesses remain legally compliant while supporting their operational and strategic objectives.

Our services include:

  • Company incorporation and business structuring
  • Corporate governance and regulatory compliance
  • Drafting, review, and negotiation of commercial agreements and contracts
  • Shareholders', partnership, joint venture, and investment agreements
  • Employment law advisory and HR legal support
  • Drafting and review of employment contracts, appointment letters, HR policies, employee handbooks, and service rules
  • POSH compliance, including policy drafting, Internal Committee (IC) constitution, legal advisory, training, and inquiry assistance
  • Employee disciplinary proceedings, misconduct inquiries, and termination-related matters
  • Labour and industrial law compliance and dispute resolution
  • Vendor, supply, service, confidentiality (NDA), and consultancy agreements
  • Mergers, acquisitions, business restructuring, and legal due diligence
  • Commercial litigation, arbitration, and dispute resolution
  • Ongoing legal advisory and compliance support for startups, MSMEs, and corporate entities
  • Trademark, copyright or patent related compliances and issues

Our objective is to provide practical, business-oriented legal solutions that minimize legal risks, strengthen compliance frameworks, and enable organizations to focus on growth with confidence.
